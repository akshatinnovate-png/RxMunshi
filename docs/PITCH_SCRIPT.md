# RxMunshi: pitch script (13 slides, about 5 minutes)

**Bold** lines are the ones to land word for word. The story is an illustrative composite, so tell it as "picture a chemist", never as a real person.

---

**1 · Cover (20 s)**
Picture Rakesh ji, a chemist in Sakchi, Jamshedpur. Every month he throws away medicines he has already paid for. Nobody stole them. They just expired.
**"India's chemists don't lose money to thieves. They lose it to the calendar."**
We are RxMunshi: an AI munshi that gets that money back.

**2 · The money leak (30 s)**
Rakesh ji loses about ₹15,000 a month: to expired stock, to wholesaler billing errors, to refill patients who drift away, and to hours spent writing registers.
We charge ₹1,030 a month.
**"We don't cost the chemist money. We find it for him: about fourteen rupees back for every one he pays us."**

**3 · Customers (20 s)**
We start with independent chemists in Tier 2 and 3 towns: the most pain, and the least software.
We reach them through the wholesaler's salesman, who already visits every shop every week.
**"We don't knock on 8 lakh doors. We ride with the man who already does."**

**4 · Market (20 s)**
About 8 lakh pharmacies, at ₹12,360 a year each, is a ₹1,000 crore market.
In year 5 we need 17,000 shops, about 6% of the reachable market, for ₹25 crore ARR.
**"We need one shop in sixteen, not the whole country."**

**5 · Lean canvas (15 s)**
One line matters: **"Other apps record your pharmacy. RxMunshi gets your money back."**

**6 · Pricing (20 s)**
There's a free plan to start the habit, and paid plans from ₹799 to ₹1,499 a month, about ₹1,030 on average.
The free user converts on the day of their first return slip, when they see real rupees come back.

**7 · Unit economics (20 s)**
It costs us ₹241 a month to serve a shop, so gross margin is 77%.
Customer acquisition pays back in 7.6 months, and lifetime value is 8.8 times what it costs to acquire a shop.
**"Software margins, a chemist's thrift."**

**8 · Year 1 (20 s)**
The year-1 goal is 600 paying shops, ₹74 lakh ARR, and ₹1.6 crore recovered for chemists.
**"Our first KPI isn't our revenue. It's theirs."**

**9 · Five years (20 s)**
₹25 crore ARR by year 5, EBITDA-positive from year 4, and peak burn of ₹7.4 crore.

**10 · Ten years (15 s)**
80,000 pharmacies, one in ten in India, and ₹160 crore ARR.
**"Every Indian chemist deserves a munshi. We'll start with one in ten."**

**11 · Porter's five forces (20 s)**
Billing software is crowded. Money-back software is empty.
**"Billing apps tell you what you sold. We tell you what you're about to lose, and get it back."**
Our moat is the data we build up on every wholesaler's prices and return terms, plus the wholesaler channel itself.

**12 · Kano and MoSCoW (20 s)**
Chemists expect the H1 register. They get delighted when they ask in Hindi, "teen wale batch return kar do", and it's done after one tap to approve.
Just as important is what we will never build: **"We will never sell a single tablet. That's why chemists trust us, and regulators don't worry about us."**

**13 · Compliance and the ask (30 s)**
The H1 register, the DPDP Act and licensed returns are built into the product, not bolted on.
We're raising ₹6 crore for 24 months, to reach 2,500 shops and ₹3.2 crore ARR.
Close on the story: **"Next month, when Rakesh ji's medicines expire, he won't throw them away. Munshi will send them back, and he'll get his money."**
Thank you.
