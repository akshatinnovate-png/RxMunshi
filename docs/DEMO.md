# Demo and build days

## The demo (3 minutes)

**Setup:** a fictional shop, "Sharma General Store", with 90 days of sales and real-looking distributor bills.

1. **Capture (30 s):** photograph a distributor bill; it becomes stock lines to confirm. Say *"aaj 12 Maggi, 6 Parle-G becha"*; sales recorded.
2. **Plan (30 s):** "Good morning, your plan is ready": three items may run out before the next delivery; the plan is Rs 600 over the Rs 5,000 budget; lower-priority lines are marked.
3. **Approve (20 s):** remove one line, approve the rest.
4. **Execute (20 s):** the order opens in WhatsApp to the distributor, pre-filled in Hindi; the task shows "sent, awaiting delivery".
5. **Verify (40 s):** photograph the delivered bill: "2 cartons short on Maggi, price of Parle-G changed". Records update; a follow-up message is drafted.
6. **Audit (20 s):** the timeline shows each step, its evidence and who approved it.
