RxMunshi 1.0.0

RxMunshi is back-office software for licensed retail pharmacies. It keeps
records, prepares work for your approval, and prints documents. It never sells,
stocks, delivers or lists medicines.

Please read before installing:

1. This Windows app runs in Demo mode. It comes with a synthetic shop (made-up
   people, phone numbers and licence numbers) and keeps all records on this
   computer only. Nothing is uploaded.
2. The compliance score, the H1 register checks and every suggestion are aids
   for the owner and the registered pharmacist. They are not legal or medical
   advice. The pharmacist decides every dispensing and substitution.
3. WhatsApp messages open in WhatsApp for you to send. Message a patient only
   after they have given consent (DPDP Act 2023).
4. The software is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.
